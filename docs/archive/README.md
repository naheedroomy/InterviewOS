# AnswerCue Documentation Archive

> **Archive notice:** Files under `docs/archive/` are historical evidence, not active requirements or current status. Filenames retain their original historical context and may reference superseded product names, paths, or claims. Nothing in this archive overrides the canonical documents in `docs/` or the repository root.

This directory preserves dated engineering reports, testing reports, and superseded root-level analysis, audit, QA, and research documents. They are kept for historical traceability and are not maintained as current guidance.

## Indexes

- [engineering/](engineering/README.md) — historical engineering reports (parity, macOS production, screenshot/screen analysis, encryption design).
- [testing/](testing/README.md) — historical testing reports (E2E results, test plans, performance and diagnostics findings).
- [root/](root/README.md) — superseded root-level audit, QA, research, and roadmap reports.

## Policy

- Archived files are evidence only; they must not override canonical documents.
- Historical filenames are preserved as-is; internal references inside archived reports may point to paths that have since moved.
- New dated reports and superseded findings are archived here rather than merged into canonical documents.
